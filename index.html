Absolutely! We never access your personal data during repair. Our technicians are trained to focus only on hardware issues. For screen repairs, your data remains completely safe.

We use high-quality OEM (Original Equipment Manufacturer) equivalent parts that match original specifications. All parts come with 6-month warranty.

We specialize in water damage repair! The sooner you bring it, the better. We'll diagnose and give you an honest assessment before any repair.
